Our Story

A Builder's Standard in Everything We Touch

CRA Construction is a Little Rock-based general contractor specializing in custom residential construction, multifamily development, and high-end interior finish-out. We build with precision, operate with integrity, and deliver work that holds up long after the ribbon is cut.

We keep the team small and senior on purpose. The people you meet at the start of your build are the same people on site through the final walkthrough — no handoffs, no layers between you and the decisions that shape your home.
